Module 4 of 10

The Module 9 workshop showed where the trust boundaries are; Module 10 asks what our AI systems should trust at each one. A trust boundary is any point where trust or ownership changes hands. This module covers how we decide to trust a user, an agent, a piece of content, a tool, data or a supplier at each boundary, and how we keep checking while the system runs.
