MODULE 11

Attack Surface & Risk Landscape

5 of 10

Concept tested: the attacker side of agentic AI. Anthropic disclosed the campaign in November 2025, attributing it with high confidence to a Chinese state-sponsored group; humans intervened only at a few decision points, and the AI sometimes hallucinated results. The Mexico case was publicly reported in February 2026. Model answer. 1) Response times measured in hours, manual triage of every alert, and patch cycles measured in weeks. 2) Automated containment for well-understood patterns: isolate a device, revoke a token, block an indicator. 3) Actions with major business impact, such as shutting down a production system or notifying regulators. Leadership point: this is the business case for Module 14. Defence has to move at a speed matched to the attack.
