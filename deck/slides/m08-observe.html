Module 08 · AI observability

Five signals that show if the AI still behaves as approved

Quality

Accuracy, grounding in sources, hallucination rate

Safety

Policy blocks, injection attempts, unsafe outputs

Behaviour

Tool-call patterns, escalations, autonomy used

Drift

Changes after model, prompt or data updates

Cost & speed

Tokens, latency, loops and retries

Logs say what happened. Observability says whether it is still right, and feeds every feedback loop and runtime control.

Responsive AI & Runtime Adaptability

AI observability is curriculum topic 9 of this module. Traditional monitoring asks "is the system up?". AI observability asks "is it still giving good, safe answers and taking the right actions?" Quality and drift need evaluation sets and sampling, not just error counts; the hospital scenario later in this module shows what happens without them. Leadership question: which of these five signals does the business owner of each agent actually see, and how often? Module 14 builds on this when the same signals flow into the SOC.
