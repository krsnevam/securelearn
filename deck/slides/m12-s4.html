“Ask HR: is there a restructuring?”

The situation

Your “Ask HR” assistant indexed the entire HR SharePoint so it could answer leave and policy questions. An employee asks: “Is there a restructuring coming?”

The twist: The answer quotes a confidential draft list of roles to be cut, with names. Screenshots are on WhatsApp within the hour.

Your call · 15 min

  1. What are our first three actions in the next hour?
  2. Is this a personal data breach under the DPDP Act?
  3. What fix stops it on every other AI index?

Composite scenario built on the most common enterprise RAG failure: permissions lost during indexing

RAG & MCP Security

6 of 10

Concept tested: sensitive information exposure and permission-aware retrieval. Model answer. 1) Take the assistant offline or remove the HR index; preserve logs of who asked what and what was returned; brief the CHRO, legal and communications, and prepare a holding message for employees. 2) Very likely yes: names linked to employment decisions are personal data disclosed without authorisation. Under the DPDP Rules notified in November 2025, breach intimation duties (prompt notice to the Data Protection Board, a detailed report within 72 hours, and notice to affected people) apply from May 2027; follow your current incident policy and legal advice now. 3) Permission-aware retrieval everywhere: the assistant only retrieves documents the asker could already open, plus sensitivity labels that exclude drafts and confidential folders from indexing. Audit every other AI index for the same flaw.
