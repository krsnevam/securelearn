Module 08 · Adaptive risk controls

Controls tighten as risk signals rise

Normal

Routine request from a known user

Act autonomously and log

Elevated

Unusual volume, new data source or new tool

Add verification; sample for review

High

Sensitive data or high-value action

Require human approval

Critical

Signs of attack or policy breach

Stop, isolate, alert the SOC

The same agent can sit at different levels within one task. Risk is scored per action, not per system.

Responsive AI & Runtime Adaptability

2 of 10

Static controls are either too tight, so people work around them, or too loose. Adaptive controls let the agent move fast on routine work and slow down automatically when the stakes or the warning signs rise. The last line is the key insight: risk is evaluated on every action, so a low-risk agent can still take one high-risk step.
