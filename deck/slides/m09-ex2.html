3 of 10

Exercise 2 from the curriculum. The user count and sources are illustrative. Strong teams will find: wikis and tickets are writeable by many people, so the knowledge base can be poisoned; document permissions may be lost at indexing, so HR or finance content leaks (see the "Ask HR" scenario in Module 12); multi-source retrieval mixes trusted policy with untrusted tickets; and nobody owns the freshness of indexed content. Recommended controls: source vetting and owners, sensitivity labels, permission-aware retrieval, citation of sources in every answer, and a knowledge governance board that approves new sources. Module 12 returns to these risks in depth with a six-control checklist, so this exercise doubles as a preview.
