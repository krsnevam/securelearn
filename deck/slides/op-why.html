OPENING CHALLENGE

Project NEXUS · fail first, then learn

This is the "wow" moment; land it calmly. The message is not that governance is useless. It is that agentic AI moves risk into architecture, so governance leaders must learn to read and challenge architecture. Ask each group to compare its signed sheet with this table: how many of their top three risks appear here? Groups that rejected NEXUS should explain what they spotted; praise the reasoning, not just the outcome. Close with: "For the rest of this program, your group owns NEXUS. You will rebuild it, module by module, and present version 2 to the board at the capstone."
