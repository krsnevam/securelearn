Real case · Oct 2025

The AI-drafted report that cost a refund

What happened

Deloitte Australia agreed to refund part of an AU$440,000 government review after an academic found fabricated references and an invented court quote. The corrected version disclosed that generative AI had been used in drafting.

Your situation: Your strategy team uses GenAI to draft board papers and client reports. Nobody tracks which documents used AI, and nobody checks citations.

Your call · 15 min

  1. Do we disclose AI use to clients and the board?
  2. Who verifies every fact and citation before release?
  3. What changes in our sign-off policy on Monday?

Source: public reporting (Associated Press, The Guardian), October 2025

MODULE 07

Responsible AI Frameworks

1 of 10

Principle tested: transparency and accountability. Model answer. 1) Yes: disclose material AI use in client deliverables and board papers; many clients now ask for it in contracts. 2) The named author, not the tool, verifies every reference and quote; for high-stakes documents add a second reviewer. 3) A one-page policy: record AI use per document, ban AI-generated citations that are not checked against the source, and require the signatory to confirm verification. Discussion hook: hallucinated citations are a reputational risk, not just a quality issue, because they look like dishonesty. Ask: how many documents left your organization last month with unchecked AI content?
