Module 12 · Data leakage

RAG can quietly bypass access controls

Risky design

A junior employee asks: “What are the salary bands for directors?”

HR files were indexed with no permissions attached, so the AI finds them and answers.

Secure design

Same question, same index.

Retrieval checks the asker’s permissions first. HR files are filtered out, and the AI says it has no access.

The AI should only retrieve what the person asking could already open.

RAG & MCP Security

6 of 10

This is the most common real-world RAG failure. Teams index a whole SharePoint or drive to make the assistant useful, and the access permissions on those files are lost in the process. Sensitive information exposure also includes secrets: passwords and API keys pasted into wikis become searchable through the AI. Ask: when our documents were indexed, did their permissions travel with them?
