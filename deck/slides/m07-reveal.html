Deleting a column does not delete the bias

The tempting answer · A

“Fairness through unawareness.” If the model can’t see gender, it can’t discriminate.

Why it fails

Proxies rebuild the hidden attribute, and you lose the data needed to measure the gap at all.

What works

Keep protected data for testing only; measure approvals and errors by group; set limits; monitor after launch.
