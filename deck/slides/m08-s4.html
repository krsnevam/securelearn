The silent model upgrade

The situation

A hospital chain’s AI drafts discharge summaries for doctors to sign. The vendor upgrades the model without notice. Two weeks later, a nurse spots a summary missing a drug allergy.

The twist: A quick audit finds allergies dropped in 2 of every 100 summaries. No alert fired, because nothing technically broke.

Your call · 15 min

  1. Roll back, add human checks, or both?
  2. Which feedback loop should have caught this in hours?
  3. What do we now require in the vendor contract?

MODULE 08

Responsive AI & Runtime Adaptability

2 of 10

Concept tested: self-correcting systems and real-time feedback loops. Model answer. 1) Both: pin or roll back the previous model version, and immediately add a mandatory allergy cross-check against the medication record before signature. Re-review summaries issued in the last two weeks for patients with recorded allergies. 2) An automated daily evaluation set (known cases where the right answer is known), plus a field-level check comparing structured data such as allergies with the generated text, with alerts on any drop. 3) Advance notice of model changes, the right to pin versions, and shared evaluation results. Leadership point: "signed by a doctor" is weak oversight if doctors sign 60 summaries a shift; design the check, do not assume it.
