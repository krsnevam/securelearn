MODULE 09

Architecture Review Workshop

3 of 10

These are the three workshop deliverables named in the curriculum. For a 3-hour session, keep each to one or two pages; the sample answer table earlier in the module is a good model for Deliverable 2. Deliverable 3 should end with a clear verdict: approve, approve with conditions, or not ready. Have each table present its executive recommendation in two minutes, as if to the CIO and CRO. These artifacts mirror what Cyber Security Risk Advisory teams produce before a production go-live decision.
