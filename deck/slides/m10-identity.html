The shared service account is the most common shortcut in early agent deployments, because it is quick to set up. It means any user, or any attacker who can steer the agent, effectively gets admin rights. Identity propagation, also called on-behalf-of delegation, fixes this: the downstream system sees the real user and applies their permissions.
