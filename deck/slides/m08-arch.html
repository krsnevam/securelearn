Module 08 · Enterprise responsive AI architecture

Six layers that let AI adapt without losing control

1 · Context

Who is asking, which data, current trust score

2 · Reasoning

Model plans the next step, with a confidence score

3 · Policy engine

Rules as code; adaptive risk thresholds

4 · Action gateway

Tool calls, approval gates, rate limits

5 · Observability

Telemetry, evaluations, drift detection

6 · Human escalation

On-call owner, override, kill switch

The feedback loop

Layer 5 feeds layers 1 and 3, so controls tighten automatically when signals worsen.

Layers 3 and 4 sit outside the model, so the AI cannot talk its way past them.

This is the summary slide for the module: the enterprise responsive AI architecture, curriculum topic 12. Each layer maps to an earlier slide: context-aware AI (layer 1), dynamic decision making and self-correction (layer 2), runtime policy enforcement and adaptive risk controls (layers 3 and 4), AI observability and feedback loops (layer 5), runtime intervention and human escalation paths (layer 6). Use it as a checklist when a vendor presents an agent platform: ask them to point to each layer in their product. Layers 3 and 4 are the ones most often missing in early deployments.
