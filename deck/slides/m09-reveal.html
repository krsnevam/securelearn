A long list is not a decision

The tempting answer · A

“Completeness protects us.” Nobody can say we missed anything.

Why it fails

Executives can’t act on 40 findings. The critical three drown among cosmetic ones, and launch goes ahead unchanged.

What works

Lead with paths to irreversible harm (money out, data out, records changed), a verdict and dated conditions. The rest goes in an appendix.
