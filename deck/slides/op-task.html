OPENING CHALLENGE

Project NEXUS · fail first, then learn

Form five groups of four to six and assign roles deliberately: put compliance-minded participants in the business owner role, and business-minded participants in the CISO role, so people argue from unfamiliar positions. These five groups stay together for the whole program and own NEXUS through every module. Timing: 5 minutes reading the exhibits, 15 minutes debate, 5 minutes writing and signing. Collect the signed sheets before the reveal; the signatures make the result personal. Walk the room and listen for which items from the governance pack each table relies on, and quote them back during the reveal.
