The OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications was published on 9 December 2025 by the OWASP GenAI Security Project, built with more than 100 practitioners. It complements the older OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, which covers model-level risks, and the OWASP MCP Top 10 for the tool-connection layer. Recommend that every AI architecture review and vendor questionnaire maps to this list; it is fast becoming the default vocabulary for auditors and procurement.
