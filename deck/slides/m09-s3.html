Realistic scenario · Insurance

Five flaws, fifteen minutes, go-live Friday

The situation

A motor insurer’s claims agent reads customer photos and PDFs, checks the policy database and pays claims up to ₹1.5 lakh through the payments API.

The twist: It runs on one shared admin account. Its prompt says “never pay fraudulent claims”. It logs only errors. The CEO has announced the launch.

Your call · 15 min

  1. Find five design flaws.
  2. Rank them by business impact.
  3. Go, go with conditions, or stop?

Composite scenario for the architecture review workshop; figures are illustrative

MODULE 09

Architecture Review Workshop

3 of 10

Concept tested: the eight review questions from earlier in this module. Answer key, at least seven flaws: (1) customer uploads are untrusted and can carry hidden text instructions; (2) a shared admin account gives the agent far more power than a claims handler; (3) the fraud rule lives in the prompt, not in code; (4) payouts up to ₹1.5 lakh with no human threshold; (5) error-only logs mean no audit trail of decisions; (6) no rate limit on payouts per hour or per policy; (7) no tested kill switch. Strong answer on question 3: go with conditions. Launch in "recommend" mode with a human approving payouts above a low threshold, a dedicated agent identity, full decision logging, and payout rate limits; move to autonomy after 30 days of measured accuracy. Leadership point: a public launch date is not a control.
