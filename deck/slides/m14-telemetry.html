Module 14 · AI telemetry

What the SOC must see from every agent

Inputs

Prompts and uploaded content

Sources

Documents and pages retrieved

Tool calls

Which tool, with which arguments

Identities

The person and the agent, together

Policy decisions

What was allowed, blocked or escalated

Outputs

Responses and data returned

Usage

Volume, tokens and cost by agent

Errors

Failures, retries and loops

Without this telemetry, an AI incident is invisible to the SOC until a customer or regulator reports it.
