Detection engineering is the discipline of writing and tuning these rules. Leaders do not write them, but they should ask the SOC lead which of these five are live today. Most organizations have zero or one. Behaviour baselines matter because agent attacks often look like normal tool use, just in an unusual sequence or volume.
