Secure Productization & Lifecycle

7 of 10

Traditional software testing checks that features work. AI testing must also check that the system cannot be made to misbehave, and that its quality has not drifted. Leaders should ask for the pass thresholds before testing starts, not after, so that "good enough" is a decision rather than a negotiation.
