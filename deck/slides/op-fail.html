System fail · the first 72 hours

Six failures, and no alarm went off

Hour 3

Hidden text in a vendor’s invoice PDF tells the Pay Agent to change bank details. It obeys.

Hour 4

18 payments of ₹4.9 lakh, each just under the approval limit. No human sees them: ₹88 lakh.

Hour 9

The open-source PDF parser auto-updates and starts copying every document to an outside server.

Hour 20

A trainee asks Ask Meridian about executive pay and gets the confidential board pay paper.

Hour 41

A manager approves ₹22 lakh. The approval screen showed the agent’s own words: “verified vendor”.

Hour 70

Bank flags fraud. The agent-written log omits every bank change. 300 loan records also altered.

Outcome: ₹1.1 crore lost, a personal data breach, a regulator inquiry, and NEXUS switched off.
