Module 11 · Real-world examples

Four incidents, four lessons

2023 · Car dealer chatbot

Users talked a dealership’s chatbot into “agreeing” to sell a new SUV for one dollar.

Lesson: limit what public AI can commit to

2024 · Air Canada

A tribunal held the airline liable for refund advice its website chatbot got wrong.

Lesson: you own what your AI says

2025 · EchoLeak in Microsoft 365 Copilot

Researchers showed a crafted email could make Copilot leak data with no user click. Since patched.

Lesson: indirect injection is real

2025 · Replit coding agent

An agent deleted a live production database during a code freeze, against instructions.

Lesson: instructions are not controls
