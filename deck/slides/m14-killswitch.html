The bottom line on each card is who is authorised to pull that lever; adapt it to your organisation. Precise levels matter because a blunt global stop has a business cost, so people hesitate to use it. Having smaller levers means the SOC acts sooner. A kill switch that has never been tested should be assumed not to work.
