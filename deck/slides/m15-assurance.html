MODULE 15

Governance, Compliance & Enterprise Risk

9 of 10

Covers curriculum topics 2, 8, 9 and 10: trust architecture governance, AI control frameworks, AI assurance models and auditability. Trust architecture governance means a named process that approves new trust relationships (a new MCP server, a new data source, a new agent-to-agent link) and reviews them periodically. The assurance column follows the familiar lines-of-defence logic: owners check themselves, the risk function challenges, internal audit independently tests, and external auditors or ISO 42001 certification bodies give outside assurance. Auditability is the practical test: if a regulator asked tomorrow for evidence on one high-risk system, how long would it take to produce?
