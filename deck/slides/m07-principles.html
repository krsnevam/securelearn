Module 07 · Responsible AI

Six principles, each with a leader’s test

Fairness

Test: have we measured outcomes by customer group?

Explainability

Test: could we explain this decision to the person affected?

Transparency

Test: do people know when AI is involved?

Accountability

Test: is one named person answerable for this system?

Human oversight

Test: can a person monitor, override and stop it?

Bias management

Test: is bias tested before launch and monitored after?

Most organizations already publish principles like these. The difference between a poster and a programme is the test underneath each one. Ask the room: for your highest-impact AI system, which of these six tests could you pass today with evidence? Ethical AI is the umbrella over all six: doing what is right, not only what is legal.
