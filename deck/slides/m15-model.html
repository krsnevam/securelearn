This maps AI onto the familiar three-lines model. The governance council is where most of the real decisions happen; it should include the business, not only technology and risk, otherwise it becomes a blocker. Model approval boards are typically a function of this council for high-risk systems. Internal audit provides independent assurance, which is what ISO 42001 certification and regulators will look for.
