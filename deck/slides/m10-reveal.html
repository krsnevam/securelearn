Borrowing the user’s rights is necessary, not enough

The tempting answer · A

“Least privilege, achieved.” It is the right instinct from traditional IT.

Why it fails

A finance manager may pay vendors. A poisoned invoice makes the agent do exactly that, with her rights.

What works

User delegation plus a task-scoped agent identity, and content trust: outside text never triggers actions.

NEXUS link: this fixes the 300 altered loan records (Hour 70). It does not stop the Hour 3 attack.

MODULE 10

Enterprise Trust Architecture

4 of 10

Key insight: identity answers "who is acting"; it does not answer "is this instruction genuine". This is the confused-deputy problem in agentic form. Identity propagation (covered later in this module) limits the blast radius; context trust stops outside content from becoming commands. Both are needed. Bridge into the module: "Now let's build the full trust architecture that NEXUS lacked." Group deliverable for this module: NEXUS v2 identity and trust-boundary map.
