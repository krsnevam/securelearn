MODULE 15

Governance, Compliance & Enterprise Risk

9 of 10

Key insight: ISO/IEC 42001 certifies an organisation's AI management system. It is valuable evidence of maturity, but it does not certify a product, and it does not establish EU AI Act conformity, which is assessed per system against the Act's requirements. Under the AI Act, deployers also carry their own obligations. Group deliverable for this module: a one-page board paper for NEXUS v2 with risk tier, EU and India obligations, residual risks and a decision request.
