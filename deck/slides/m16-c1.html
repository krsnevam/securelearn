Module 16 · Capstone case A

Realistic scenario · E-commerce

Festive sale meltdown

The situation

Hour three of your biggest sale of the year. A pricing agent reads review summaries from a RAG index to tune offers. Poisoned reviews carry hidden instructions, and 3,000 products drop to 90% off for 47 minutes.

Roles: CEO, CISO, CFO, Head of Customer Experience, Head of Communications.

Your call · 30 min

  1. Stop the agent, the sale, or both?
  2. Honour the orders already placed?
  3. Draft the first public statement in 10 minutes.

MODULE 16

Capstone · Architecture, SOC & Governance Review

10 of 10

Capstone case A integrates RAG poisoning (Module 12), indirect injection (Module 11) and runtime defence (Module 14). Facilitation: give each role a private brief. CFO: the loss is growing every minute. Head of Customer Experience: thousands of happy customers are posting their bargains. CISO: the source is not yet confirmed. Head of Communications: two journalists have called. Strong answers: stop the pricing agent (level 3) but keep the sale running with prices frozen at the last known-good values; consider honouring orders up to a value cap, or cancel with an apology and a goodwill voucher, and decide consistently; the statement should be short, factual, and say what customers will get. Debrief: which controls were missing? A price floor enforced in code, rate limits on price changes, treating reviews as untrusted, and an alert on margin anomalies.
