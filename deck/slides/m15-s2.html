Module 15 · Scenario 2 of 3

Real regulation · India 2025–26

A CEO avatar in 12 languages

What happened

India’s amended IT Rules, in force since 20 February 2026, require AI-generated content on platforms to be clearly labelled with embedded provenance. The DPDP Rules, notified in November 2025, phase in consent and security duties by May 2027.

Your situation: Marketing wants an AI avatar of the CEO speaking 12 Indian languages in customer videos, plus a chat agent that stores every conversation.

Your call · 15 min

  1. Approve, approve with conditions, or reject?
  2. What labelling and consent do we need?
  3. How long do we keep the chat transcripts?

Sources: IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Amendment Rules, notified 10 Feb 2026; DPDP Rules 2025, notified 13 Nov 2025

MODULE 15

Governance, Compliance & Enterprise Risk

9 of 10

Concept tested: the model approval board in action. Model answer. 1) Approve with conditions. 2) Label every video clearly as AI-generated (the platforms that host it must label synthetic content and keep provenance markers, and doing it yourself builds trust); get the CEO's written consent and set limits on what the avatar may say; publish a way for customers to verify official videos, because a convincing CEO avatar also makes impersonation scams easier. For the chat agent: clear notice and consent at the start, purpose limitation, and no use of conversations for model training without consent. 3) Only as long as the purpose needs, for example 90 days unless there is a complaint or a legal hold, then deletion; DPDP retention and erasure duties apply from May 2027 but build to them now. Leadership point: governance is not saying no; it is saying "yes, with these conditions".
