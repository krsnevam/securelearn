Figures here are illustrative; set your own thresholds. The most important message: telling a model "never do X" in its instructions is a request, not a control. A determined user or a malicious document can talk the model out of it. Real policy sits in a layer the model cannot rewrite, typically an AI gateway or the tool's own permission system.
