The tempting answers · A, C

“Block the bad input.” It mirrors how we fight malware and phishing.

Why it fails

Prompts are requests, not controls. Detection is probabilistic, and attackers retry until one gets through.

What works

No irreversible powers for agents that read outside content. Verify bank changes out of band. Keep detection as a second layer.
