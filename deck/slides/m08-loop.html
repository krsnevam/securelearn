Module 08 · Real-time feedback loops

Governance plugs into the loop at two points

Sense

Request, context, data signals

Decide

Model plans the next step

Act

Policy check before every action

Observe

Telemetry on every outcome

Adjust

Feeds back into the next Sense

Before acting: prevent

Rules checked outside the model decide whether the action may run.

After acting: detect

Observability shows drift, errors and misuse fast enough to intervene.

Every agent runs some version of this loop, many times per task. Leaders do not need to know the code, but they should know the two points where control lives: a policy check between deciding and acting, and observability after acting. If a vendor cannot show you both, the system is ungoverned at runtime.
