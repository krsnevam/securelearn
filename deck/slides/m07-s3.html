Realistic scenario · Banking

The PIN-code problem

The situation

A private bank’s AI pre-approves personal loans in 90 seconds. Approval rates in four districts run 18 points below the state average. The model never sees religion or caste, but it does use PIN code and phone model.

The twist: A journalist has the numbers and calls at 10 a.m. tomorrow. RBI’s FREE-AI framework expects board-approved AI policy and fairness testing.

Your call · 15 min

  1. Pause the model, or keep it running while we investigate?
  2. Which of the six principle tests did we fail?
  3. What do we tell the journalist and the customers affected?

Built on: RBI Committee report, Framework for Responsible and Ethical Enablement of AI (FREE-AI), August 2025

Responsible AI Frameworks

1 of 10

Composite scenario; the figures are fictional. Principle tested: fairness and explainability; the key concept is proxy discrimination, where neutral-looking data such as PIN code or phone model stands in for protected characteristics. Model answer. 1) Do not simply switch off: route affected districts to manual underwriting while the model is investigated, so customers are not harmed either way. 2) Fairness (outcomes not measured by region), explainability (cannot explain a decline), accountability (no owner watching outcomes). 3) Be factual: acknowledge the disparity, explain the interim manual review, commit to an independent fairness review, and offer declined applicants a re-assessment. RBI's FREE-AI report (August 2025) sets out principles and recommendations for regulated entities, including board-approved AI policies; check the latest supervisory expectations with compliance.
