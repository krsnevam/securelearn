Module 07 · Bias management

Bias enters at four points, not one

1

Data · history reflects past unfair decisions

2

Design · the goal or proxy chosen favours one group

3

Deployment · used on people it was never tested on

4

Feedback · biased outputs become tomorrow’s data

Controls to expect

  • Data review before training or retrieval
  • Fairness metrics by group
  • Testing on real user populations
  • Post-launch outcome monitoring
  • A route for people to appeal
