The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) classifies AI by use, not by technology. In 2026 the Digital Omnibus on AI postponed the high-risk obligations: stand-alone high-risk systems under Annex III moved from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027, and high-risk AI embedded in regulated products under Annex I moved from August 2027 to 2 August 2028. Prohibitions and general-purpose AI duties were not delayed. For Indian and other non-EU organizations: the Act applies if your AI system's output is used in the EU. Remind the room that the delay is a planning window, not a reprieve, and to check the final text with legal counsel.
