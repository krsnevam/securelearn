Real case · Jun 2025

The password was 123456

What happened

Security researchers logged into the admin side of McHire, McDonald’s AI hiring chatbot run by Paradox.ai, using “123456” as username and password. They could reach records of up to 64 million applicants. The vendor fixed it quickly once told.

Your situation: Procurement is about to sign an AI chatbot vendor that will hold your customers’ personal data.

Your call · 15 min

  1. Which five questions do we ask before any data flows?
  2. What evidence beats the vendor’s word?
  3. Who signs off vendor AI risk, and how often?

Source: disclosure by researchers Ian Carroll and Sam Curry, June–July 2025; Paradox.ai statement

MODULE 10

Enterprise Trust Architecture

4 of 10

Concept tested: trust in AI vendors and their supply chain. The AI itself was not the weak point; basic security around it was. Model answer. 1) Where is our data stored and for how long? Who at the vendor can access it, and how is admin access protected (MFA, no default accounts)? Is our data used to train models? What certifications and recent penetration tests exist? How fast will they notify us of a breach? 2) Independent evidence: a current ISO 27001 or SOC 2 report, a recent penetration test summary, a right-to-audit clause, and a short technical review by your own team. 3) The governance council's vendor process, re-checked yearly and on any major product change. India angle: under the DPDP Act the company that decides why data is collected (the data fiduciary) stays responsible for what its processors do.
