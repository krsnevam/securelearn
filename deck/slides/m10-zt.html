Enterprise Trust Architecture

4 of 10

Many organizations already have a Zero Trust programme for networks and identity. The point here is that agents are a new kind of actor that those programmes did not anticipate. An agent acting on behalf of a trusted employee can still be steered by untrusted content, so trust in the user does not automatically extend to everything the agent does.
