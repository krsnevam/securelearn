These figures are a worked example for a fictional mid-size bank, not real data. Read them as a board would: 78% inventory coverage means more than a fifth of AI use is unknown; 3 of 12 high-risk systems lack a current assessment; the oldest critical finding is two months old; containment in 42 minutes is good. Then ask participants which of these six they could produce for their own organization today. Boards respond to a small, stable set of numbers tracked over time, not long narrative reports. The threshold rule turns reporting into a leadership decision framework: when a number crosses its line, a pre-agreed decision is triggered.
