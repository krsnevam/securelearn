Covers curriculum topics 6, 7, 10 and 11: monitoring architecture, observability, audit trails and logging frameworks. The test at the bottom is the one auditors and regulators effectively apply; the EU AI Act, for example, requires automatic logging for high-risk systems. Audit trails must be written by the gateway and infrastructure, not by the agent itself, because an agent can omit actions from its own summary (see the scenario in Module 14). Agree retention with legal and privacy: long enough for investigations and regulation, short enough to respect data minimisation under the DPDP Act.
