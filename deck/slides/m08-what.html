Responsive AI & Runtime Adaptability

2 of 10

Adaptability is the business value of agentic AI: it handles situations nobody scripted. It is also the risk, because behaviour you approved last month may not be the behaviour you have today. The leadership job is to define the fixed boundaries, so adaptation happens inside them.
