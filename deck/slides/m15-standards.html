Governance, Compliance & Enterprise Risk

9 of 10

Leaders often fear running five compliance programmes. The practical answer is one management system, usually ISO 42001, with crosswalks to each law and framework. For Indian organizations, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 governs personal data used by AI, and the national AI governance guidelines published by MeitY are principle-based rather than a separate AI law. Sector regulators such as RBI and SEBI may add their own expectations; check the latest position with compliance teams.
