Leaders are often told to "pick a framework". These are complementary rather than competing. The NIST Generative AI profile is NIST AI 600-1, published in 2024. ISO 42001 is the one an auditor can certify you against, which matters for customers and regulators. We return to these in Module 15 alongside the EU AI Act.
