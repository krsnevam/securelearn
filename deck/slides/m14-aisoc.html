Curriculum topic 1 of this module: AI systems in SOC environments. The two sides connect: the AI Security Copilot reviewed in the Module 9 workshop is "AI for the SOC", and it is also an asset the SOC must defend. A copilot that reads attacker-controlled alert data (phishing emails, malicious URLs, log entries) can be manipulated by that data, as the GrafanaGhost research showed in 2026 for an observability assistant. Ask the room: does the SOC's own AI tooling appear in the SOC's asset inventory?
