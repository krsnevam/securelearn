Curriculum topic 14: leadership decision frameworks. The decision rights strip links to the tiering model shown earlier: the higher the tier, the more senior the decision maker. "Approve with conditions" is the most useful and most abused option; insist that every condition has a date and an owner, and that unmet conditions trigger an automatic review. Retirement is a decision too: an agent nobody owns any more should be switched off. Practice: take one AI proposal from a participant's organisation and walk it through the four decisions.
