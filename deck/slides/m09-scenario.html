Architecture Review Workshop

3 of 10

This workshop opens Component 2 of the program and applies Modules 1 to 8: architecture, agents, RAG, MCP, trust, responsible AI and responsive AI. Participants act as an Enterprise AI Security and Risk Advisory team reviewing a large organisation's proposed agentic AI platform before production. Suggested timing for the 3 hours: brief and method 20 minutes; Exercise 1 (Security Copilot) 40 minutes; Exercise 2 (Knowledge Assistant) 35 minutes; Exercise 3 (MCP Risk Advisory Platform) 35 minutes; writing and presenting the three deliverables 50 minutes. Assign each table one exercise as its lead, and rotate the others as lighter reviews if time is short.
