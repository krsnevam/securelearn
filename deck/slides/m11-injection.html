Attack Surface & Risk Landscape

5 of 10

Prompt injection is ranked the top risk for LLM applications by OWASP, and goal hijack is first in the OWASP agentic list. The illustrative scenario shows why indirect injection is the bigger business risk: the attacker never logs in, they just send content. No model is fully immune today, so the defence is architectural: limit what the agent can do after reading untrusted content, and require approval for sensitive actions such as sending data outside the company.
