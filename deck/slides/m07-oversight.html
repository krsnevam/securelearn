Module 07 · Human oversight

Match autonomy to impact

Less autonomy · higher impact

More autonomy · lower impact

Human in the loop

A person approves each action before it happens.

Payments, hiring, credit, legal notices

Human on the loop

AI acts; a person monitors and can step in.

Refunds under a limit, scheduling

Human out of the loop

AI acts alone, inside hard guardrails.

Ticket triage, internal search

Rule of thumb: autonomy should shrink as impact and irreversibility grow.

Responsible AI Frameworks

Human-in-the-loop versus autonomous execution is the single most important design choice a leader signs off. Two questions decide it: how much harm if the action is wrong, and can it be undone? A refund can be reversed; a leaked dataset cannot. Warn about rubber-stamping: a human who approves 500 actions a day is no longer really overseeing.
