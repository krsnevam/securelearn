Module 13 · Compliance validation & operational security reviews

Validate before launch, then review on a rhythm

Compliance validation · before go-live

  • Risk classification confirmed (EU AI Act, internal tier)
  • Privacy: notice, consent, retention, transfers
  • Sector rules checked (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI)
  • Required documentation and logging in place
  • Evidence filed for audit

Operational security reviews · after go-live

  • Monthly: access, tokens and tool permissions
  • Monthly: incidents, near misses, overrides
  • Quarterly: red-team retest and drift review
  • On every change: re-validate before release
  • Yearly: full re-approval by risk tier

Covers two topics from the Module 13 list: compliance validation and operational security reviews. Compliance validation is the go-live gate that confirms legal and regulatory obligations are met with evidence; it feeds the compliance row of the production readiness assessment. Operational security reviews keep the system safe after launch, because agents, tools, data and threats keep changing. The cadence shown is a suggested starting point; scale it by risk tier. For NEXUS, a monthly access review would have caught the shared admin account, and a change re-validation would have caught the auto-updating PDF parser.
