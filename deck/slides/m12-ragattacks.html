Module 12 · RAG security

Every stage of the RAG pipeline can be attacked

1 · Ingest

Knowledge poisoning

Planted or altered documents enter the knowledge base

2 · Embed

Embedding manipulation

Text crafted to match many questions and rank first

3 · Store

Vector DB attacks

An exposed vector store is read, copied or altered

4 · Retrieve

Retrieval hijacking

Attacker content wins the search over the truth

5 · Generate

Context injection

Retrieved text carries hidden instructions

Research has shown that a handful of planted documents can be enough to steer answers on a targeted topic.

RAG & MCP Security

6 of 10

Walk the pipeline left to right. The key leadership insight: RAG is often sold as the safe way to use company data, but whoever can write to the knowledge base can influence the AI's answers. That includes shared drives, wikis and ticketing systems that many people can edit. The final line refers to academic work such as PoisonedRAG (2024), which showed a few injected texts could reliably steer answers to targeted questions.
