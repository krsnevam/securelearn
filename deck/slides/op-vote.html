Count the decisions aloud and write the tally on a flipchart. Expect most tables to choose A or B; conditions are usually about monitoring, a phased rollout or more testing. Then ask one question: "Which of your conditions would have changed the architecture?" Pause. Then reveal: whatever the committee decided, the board approved NEXUS with conditions and it went live.
