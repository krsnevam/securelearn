Boston Institute of Analytics · Agentic AI Security, Architecture & Governance

Every module ends with three or four scenarios before its leadership discussion. Each takes about 15 minutes. Seat participants in tables of four or five and rotate roles: business owner, CISO, legal and compliance, operations, communications. Tags tell participants how to read each case: real cases are documented incidents with a source on the slide; research findings were demonstrated by security researchers but not necessarily exploited in the wild; realistic scenarios are composites set in Indian enterprises, built from real attack patterns, and use no real company names. If time is short, run one scenario per module and set the rest as pre-reading.
