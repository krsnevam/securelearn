Why this matters to leaders

When AI can act, every AI risk becomes an operating risk.

Assistants suggest. Agents read our data, call our systems and make decisions, at machine speed and at scale.

This is the core message of the program. A chatbot that gives a wrong answer is an embarrassment; an agent that takes a wrong action can move money, delete records or leak data. That shifts AI risk from the innovation team into operational, legal and reputational risk, which is the territory of this room.
