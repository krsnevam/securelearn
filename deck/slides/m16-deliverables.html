These are the capstone deliverables exactly as listed in the curriculum. The SOC exercise produces two documents: the SOC Investigation Report (incident timeline, attack analysis, detection findings, root cause analysis, containment actions, recovery actions, lessons learned) and the Executive Risk Briefing (business impact summary, governance findings, risk assessment, strategic recommendations, future security roadmap). Share this slide at the start of the capstone so teams plan their time around the outputs, not the analysis.
