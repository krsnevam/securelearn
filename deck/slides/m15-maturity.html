Governance, Compliance & Enterprise Risk

9 of 10

An AI control maturity assessment gives the leadership team a shared, honest picture. Scoring each domain separately avoids a misleading average: an organization can be level 3 on governance and level 1 on monitoring. Ask each participant to score their own organization quickly; the spread of answers in the room is itself a useful finding. Agree a 12-month target per domain.
