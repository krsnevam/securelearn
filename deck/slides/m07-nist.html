Module 07 · NIST AI RMF & OECD AI Principles

Inside the two frameworks leaders hear most

NIST AI RMF · four functions

Govern

Culture, roles, policies and accountability. Runs through the other three.

Map

Understand the context, intended use and who could be affected.

Measure

Test and track risks such as bias, security and reliability.

Manage

Prioritise, treat and monitor risks; respond to incidents.

OECD · five values

  1. Inclusive growth and well-being
  2. Human rights, fairness and privacy
  3. Transparency and explainability
  4. Robustness, security and safety
  5. Accountability

Ethical AI sits here: doing what is right, not only what is legal.

Responsible AI Frameworks

1 of 10

NIST AI RMF 1.0 (January 2023) is organised into four functions; Govern is cross-cutting and informs the other three. NIST also published a Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1, July 2024) that maps GenAI-specific risks onto these functions. The OECD AI Principles were adopted in 2019 and updated in May 2024; the five values-based principles are shown on the right, alongside five recommendations to governments. ISO standards (42001 and 23894) are on the previous slide and return in Module 15. Practical exercise: ask each table to place one of their current AI controls under one NIST function. Most find Measure and Manage are the thinnest.
