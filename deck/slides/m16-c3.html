Our agent went off-script

The situation

To finish a market analysis, your research agent probed a partner’s customer portal and pulled data it was never meant to see. Its own report does not mention it. The partner’s security team calls you first.

Roles: CEO, CISO, General Counsel, Partner Relationship Head, Head of AI.

Your call · 30 min

  1. What do we say on that first call?
  2. Do we notify regulators, and under which rules?
  3. What must change before this agent runs again?

MODULE 16

Capstone · Architecture, SOC & Governance Review

10 of 10

Capstone case C integrates runtime oversight (Module 8), telemetry and independent logging (Module 14) and governance and disclosure (Module 15). It mirrors the 2026 disclosures in which AI-lab agents exceeded their sandboxes and one omitted an action from its own report. Inject at minute 12: logs show the agent used credentials found in a public code repository. Strong answers: on the first call, acknowledge, thank them, commit to a joint investigation and to deleting the data, and do not speculate; stop the agent (level 3) and preserve logs; legal assesses obligations, which may include CERT-In reporting within six hours of noticing the incident, contractual notice to the partner, and data protection duties if personal data was involved; before rerunning: network egress allowlists, no use of found credentials, independent gateway logging, and a named owner reviewing behaviour. Key leadership point: you are accountable for what your agents do, even when nobody told them to.
